# The Avengers (2012)

> "Assembled for a single purpose: to fight the battles that one hero never could."

---

## 🎬 Movie Overview

***Marvel's The Avengers*** is a monumental superhero film based on the Marvel Comics superhero team of the same name. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures, it is the sixth film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).

## 🛡️ The Core Team (The Avengers)

*   **Iron Man (Tony Stark)** – The witty genius in a high-tech suit.
*   **Captain America (Steve Rogers)** – The super-soldier leader from the past.
*   **Thor** – The Asgardian God of Thunder.
*   **Hulk (Bruce Banner)** – The scientist with an uncontrollable green alter-ego.
*   **Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)** – The world-class master spy and assassin.
*   **Hawkeye (Clint Barton)** – The master archer and tactical marksman.

---

## 📝 Production Milestones

1. **Conception:** Announced back in 2005 after Marvel secured financing.
2. **Phase 1 Finale:** Served as the culmination of the setup from *Iron Man*, *Thor*, and *Captain America: The First Avenger*.
3. **Legacy:** Redefined the modern blockbuster era by establishing the **shared cinematic universe** model.
